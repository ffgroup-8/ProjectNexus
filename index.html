🗂️ Nexus Entertainment District — Master Knowledge Base
Compiled from: accepted Phase 1 proposal (sent Aug 21, 2026, accepted Aug 31, 2026).
Project Overview
  • Working Name: Nexus (official naming is a Phase 1 deliverable)
  • What it is: A new entertainment and sports district in Tulsa, Oklahoma
  • Location: Tulsa, Oklahoma
  • Engagement: Phase 1, brand identity, naming, and launch systems
  • Timeline: September 14 to November 2, 2026, three milestone tiers (rescheduled from the proposal's original August 27 start)
  • Investment: $48,500 total ($17,000 Tier 1, $17,000 Tier 2, $14,500 Tier 3)
  • Key contacts: blake@luckdragon.net (Luckdragon) · elliot@mcnellies.com (McNellie's Group)
Phase 1 Deliverables
  • Brand Strategy, Naming & Identity: naming exploration, moodboards, sketches, primary and secondary logo systems, responsive identity variations, typography and color systems, foundational brand guidelines
  • Figma Brand & Design Library: centralized library of brand assets, color variables, type styles, grids, graphic treatments, and core UI components; the design source of truth for all future phases
  • Print & Promotional Collateral: business cards, postcards, information sheets, promotional handouts, stakeholder materials, and reusable print templates (commercial printing and fabrication not included)
  • Promotional Website: custom one-page responsive site on Next.js and Sanity CMS with modular content structure, multi-faceted inquiry form, SEO foundation, analytics, and production deployment
  • Presentation System: flexible Figma slide library for stakeholder, partner, investor, and development presentations
Key Constraints & Notes
  • The identity must scale across future zones, venues, sub-brands, events, and district extensions
  • Physical signage, wayfinding, environmental graphics, fabrication, and installation are a separate future phase; Phase 1 establishes the visual system they build from
  • Google Drive organization and weekly backups are part of the standard process
  • Agentic web standards ship by default: llms.txt, structured data, semantic architecture, AI crawler guidance

How This Tracker Works:

1. Phases: Work is delivered in 3 milestone phases. The phase pill shows where the project is today; the overall donut is the weighted total across all phases.

2. Editing: Open the live URL with #edit on the end to tick milestone checkboxes, then click Copy progress and paste it to Claude to publish. The plain URL stays read-only for the client.

3. Status updates: To mark a page category done (Design, Dev, SEO, Content, Sanity, Created), tell Claude the page and category. Claude flips the pill ○ → ◐ → ✓ and the progress recalculates automatically.

4. Content strategy: Each page in the content section uses the box hierarchy — plain-text Purpose on top, then the green SEO/AEO box, then the blue Content Created box. The two boxes are siblings, never nested.

5. Progress: Never remove progress already marked complete. Progress only moves forward unless you say to reset.
